How this estimate works

  • Baseline: season or career average entered by you.
  • Recent form: recent average entered by you, weighted 40% in this demo.
  • Projection: baseline × 60% + recent average × 40%.
  • Difference: projection minus the entered line. A positive value is an over lean; negative is an under lean.
  • Not a probability: the size of the difference is not the chance of winning. This demo does not estimate calibrated probabilities or account for sportsbook vig.

Data & model status

The dashboard is a working front-end prototype. Accurate automated projections require a reliable data pipeline, historical game logs, opponent/context features, line snapshots, and backtesting. Free sources may not provide licensed sportsbook odds or complete prop markets.
