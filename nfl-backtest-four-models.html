Important interpretation notes

Each test uses only earlier weeks for player averages and defensive adjustments. Defensive context is estimated from the selected position/stat's earlier opponent performances, not future games. Predictions are grouped by player ID and sorted by season/week; duplicate entries are removed. The position model is the same historical-volume × efficiency approach used in the position-specific projection page, blended with the improved historical baseline; the models here are reconstructed for historical evaluation and may not exactly reproduce all live-page settings. The improved formula is an experimental 45% season average + 35% last five + 20% last three, shrunk toward the season average when the history is short, with a modest capped opponent adjustment. No target-game statistics enter any forecast. Opponent adjustments use prior weeks only.

Games missing stat or player identity are skipped. All-recorded-games evaluation includes zero-stat player games present in the data; players absent from the weekly file cannot be evaluated. The active-usage threshold selects games after the fact and may overstate accuracy. Reusing the same seasons to tune the model can cause overfitting. This dashboard measures player-stat error, not historical DraftKings profitability or betting win rate.
