Backtest settings

Original model = average of the player's earlier games this season. Improved model = 45% season average + 35% last five + 20% last three, blended toward season average until six games; optional opponent adjustment uses only earlier defense-game totals and is capped at ±15% with 25% strength. Missing defensive samples mean no adjustment.

How to interpret this

Only QB games with 10+ attempts are scored, so the reported accuracy does not describe all QB starts, including early injury exits. Position and attempts must be present in the input CSV. A lower error in this backtest does not guarantee future accuracy. Player availability, injuries, weather, role changes, and roster movement aren't explicitly modeled. Comparing many players and weeks can overweight players who appear more often. The opponent adjustment uses a rough all-player defense total; it is not position-specific. This is a retrospective evaluation, not an independently validated betting edge.
