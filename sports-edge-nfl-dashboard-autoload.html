Baseline combines historical production with position-specific volume and efficiency estimates when enough data is available. Matchup factor compares the selected defense’s per-game allowed stat against the league average for the same season. Only part of that factor is applied, with a cap of ±15%. This is an experimental heuristic, not a calibrated probability. Injuries, weather and expected playing time are not modeled. Model selection is based on exploratory historical tests and needs future holdout validation.
